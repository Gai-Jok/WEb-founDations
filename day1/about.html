About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple tool for jotting down ideas before they slip your mind. It lets you quickly add short notes and see them listed right away, with no sign-up or clutter in the way.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open QuickNotes in your browser.
  2. Type your note into the "New note" box.
  3. Click "Add Note" to save it to your list.
  4. Check the "Your notes" section to see everything you have saved.

Features

  • Fast, distraction-free note entry
  • A running count of how many notes you have
  • A clean list view of all your notes
  • Works entirely in your browser

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Enter Add the note
